# Hyde logo asset notes

The original uploads are stored unchanged in `assets/originals/`.

The two root-level PNGs are transparent-background wordmark exports derived from those supplied files:

- `hyde-wordmark-black.png` — black artwork for white/light backgrounds.
- `hyde-wordmark-white.png` — white artwork for black/dark backgrounds.

Use `object-fit: contain` and preserve the natural aspect ratio. The transparent files are intended for the website header/footer. Keep the original square artworks for social graphics or brand moments where the full framed lockup is desired.
